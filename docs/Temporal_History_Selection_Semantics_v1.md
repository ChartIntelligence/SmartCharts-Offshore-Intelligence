## Three identities and non-inflation

Environmental observation identity must reuse qualified provider/product/revision, represented support, spatial sample and immutable frame/archive/content identity as applicable. Same frame alone is not sufficient to identify one spatial sample; same timestamp alone cannot identify a revision. No global observation-ID contract is created here.

Assessment identity is interpretation of exact evidence under assessmentAt and evaluator/governance configuration. Publication identity binds a cycle and its frozen inputs. Neither is a new environmental observation. Reassessing one NOAA frame six times per day must not automatically multiply observational support. Keep these assessments as as-used interpretation records; do not feed them as independent represented observations.

The tested existing persistence consumer deduplicates snapshotId, not archive/sample identity. Two differently named records for the same represented observation plus a later observation produce three samples instead of two. This is a demonstrated input-boundary risk, not a formula defect. Repeated provider values at distinct legitimate represented times remain meaningful: equal values must not be deduplicated. Exact duplicate, semantic duplicate, revision and repeated value are distinct categories.

## Selection dimensions still requiring governance

Represented support: instant, interval/composite, unknown and static distinctions remain governed by existing Frame contracts. Timestamp-only consumers do not establish safe comparison of different support classes. Daily-labelled evidence is not automatically a known daily support interval. Static bathymetry is carried context, not repeated temporal observations; a static-only fixture cannot supply SST history.

Revisions: preserve original and corrected evidence separately in immutable archive. Same-support revisions are not independent elapsed observations. As-used publications remain bound to their original revision. A future retrospective best-evidence path needs explicit revision precedence, admissibility and lineage; no preference for corrected/NRT/REP is invented, and no new product is qualified.

Gaps/outages: synthetic 240-hour gap is CALCULABLE in SST, not SCIENTIFICALLY_QUALIFIED. No forward-fill, fabricated update, interpolation or extra T0 sample is inserted. Confidence need not account for elapsed gap merely because it uses sample count. Upstream family admissibility/qualification is required; stale/unavailable cannot be silently made qualified. Maximum gap, outage treatment, maximum useful age and endpoint selection remain POLICY_REQUIRED.

Lookback: last-two supplied snapshots in change is a consumer-local rule, not source-selection sufficiency. Other consumers operate on supplied histories with their own gates. 48 rows is request retrieval implementation only. No universal sample count, hours/days, event window or retention selected. Minimum 2 in several consumers cannot be generalized to all 27 compositions.

As-used versus retrospective: historical reconstruction and what Pelora published use exact immutable as-used evidence and assessment. Later correction must not mutate that account. Retrospective learning may use separately governed best evidence, not silently rewrite as-used input. Current algorithms accept supplied histories; source choice is not resolved by their ability to compute. Nightly learning and captain logs remain separate.

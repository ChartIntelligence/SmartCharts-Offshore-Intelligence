A metadata modification date is not a science algorithm version. [Exact ERDDAP metadata](https://coastwatch.noaa.gov/erddap/info/noaacwBLENDEDNRTcurrentsDaily/index.html)

**PROVIDER-DOCUMENTED BEHAVIOR (P2/P3).** NOAA describes multi-mission along-track altimetry, harmonized RADS records/corrections, SLA mapping by optimal interpolation, then geostrophic currents. It specifies conventional f-plane equations and equatorial beta-plane treatment. Level-3 prose coexists with a Level-4 product listing; retain both descriptions rather than silently selecting one. NRT input latency is described as 3-5 hours while product details list 12 hours. DT uses final GDR/NTC records and is described as more accurate. These statements neither establish a daily averaging operator nor NRT vector error bars. [Product](https://coastwatch.noaa.gov/cwn/products/sea-level-anomaly-and-geostrophic-currents-multi-mission-global-optimal-interpolation.html), [RADS processing](https://coastwatch.noaa.gov/cwn/processing-algorithms/rads.html)

**AUTHORITATIVE UPSTREAM FACT (M1).** CNES-CLS13 MDT is a fixed mean-field estimate referenced to 1993-2012. Its documented role is to reference compatible SLA to absolute dynamic topography; construction combines geoid/mean-sea-surface information with in-situ mean heights and velocities through objective analysis. Matching reference periods matters. This establishes the upstream MDT role, not NOAA's exact current-release equation or reference alignment. [CNES/CLS13 description](https://www.aviso.altimetry.fr/en/data/products/auxiliary-products/mdt/mdt-global-cnes-cls13/mdt-description.html)

**AUTHORITATIVE UPSTREAM FACT (M2).** The 2013 presentation discusses observation error and correlation radius, a roughly 125-km filtered MDT first guess, subsequent smaller-scale improvement, and comparisons with independent surface velocities. Those are MDT construction/validation results, not the effective resolution, covariance or m/s uncertainty of this NOAA NRT product. No numerical MDT validation statistic is adopted as a Pelora error bound. [Mulet et al., OSTST 2013](https://mobile.aviso.altimetry.fr/fileadmin/documents/OSTST/2013/oral/mulet_MDT_CNES_CLS13.pdf)

**PEER-REVIEWED PRODUCT-FAMILY FACT (N1).** A NOAA-authored 2024 overview lists absolute dynamic topography and geostrophic surface velocity within daily RADS gridded altimetry. It describes a planned 2025 algorithm update; this is not proof the endpoint deployed it. [Bayler et al., 2024, DOI 10.3390/rs16142656](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/61767/noaa_61767_DS1.pdf)

**INFERENCE / UNRESOLVED.** Together, MDT involvement and the ADT product-family description support an absolute-current interpretation as a hypothesis. They do not establish whether this endpoint differentiates SLA+MDT, adds separately derived mean velocities, uses which reference alignment, or changed method after the published overview. Absolute versus anomaly construction remains **PROVIDER_CLARIFICATION_REQUIRED**. Static MDT can still contribute spatial gradients and systematic spatial error; static does not mean irrelevant to a derivative.

## Spatial support and Pelora geometry

**UNRESOLVED.** No exact NRT OI covariance model, spatial/temporal correlation length, influence radius, weights, background, mapping-error field, coastal stencil or effective-resolution value was established from accessible material. **EFFECTIVE_RESOLUTION_UNRESOLVED.** Neither the MDT first-guess scale nor scales from another mapped altimetry product fill this gap. Adjacent-cell independence: **NOT_ESTABLISHED**. Shared observations/information are possible in a mapped field; actual overlap and error correlation are unquantified. This is an OI-related inference, not an observed cell covariance.

**REPOSITORY FACT.** [Sampling](../backend/server.js:1343) uses a nominal 15-nautical-mile radius: N/S latitude offsets are +/-0.25 degrees; E/W longitude offsets are +/-0.25/cos(latitude). Center is requested separately. [Extraction](../backend/server.js:2345) uses parenthesized coordinates and `last` independently. ERDDAP documents closest-coordinate selection, not bilinear interpolation. Upstream OI is a different operation. [ERDDAP query rules](https://coastwatch.noaa.gov/erddap/griddap/documentation.html)

**GEOMETRIC INFERENCE; no environmental computation.** Let h=0.25 degrees and Q be nearest-grid-coordinate selection. Away from exact ties, Q(y+h)=Q(y)+h. With a consistent translation-invariant tie rule this also holds at ties. Thus N/S are one row from the center and two rows apart. In the governed 15-32 degrees N domain, the longitude radius is h/cos(latitude), strictly between h and 1.5h. E/W therefore select one or two columns from center, and two or three columns apart. They cannot select the center column. The N/S columns and E/W row equal those of center. Under the stated tie assumption, all five roles select distinct coordinates; adjacent cells and multiple-cell separation occur. This is not five independent measurements.

Exact midpoint behavior is not qualified by a live response or a deployed ERDDAP implementation audit. With inconsistent tie handling at points exactly one grid interval apart, a shared N/center or S/center coordinate is mathematically possible; it is not established as deployed behavior. Retain this narrow caveat rather than claiming unconditional source-cell independence. Different candidate locations can also share selected cells. Masks can remove otherwise distinct cells.

**GEOMETRIC INFERENCE.** Longitude arc length is proportional to cos(latitude); latitude arc length is not reduced by that factor. Therefore a 0.25-degree longitude step is physically shorter than a 0.25-degree latitude step in the Gulf, and varies across the domain. The application's offset approximation does not prove an exact geodesic stencil. Actual resolved coordinates, metric handling and rounding would need qualification later. No new offsets or estimator are chosen.

**DERIVATIVE SUPPORT = UNRESOLVED.** Distinct coordinate support makes arithmetic conceivable, but native spacing is not effective resolution. No scientifically resolved scale is approved.

## Interpretation boundary and decision

**SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION.** For ideal smooth constant-f geostrophy, mixed height derivatives cancel. Variable f can leave a geostrophic divergence term; discrete mapping, metric choice, smoothing and errors can also produce nonzero results. The previous [science qualification](NOAA_Geostrophic_Current_Divergence_Qualification_v1.md) records the authoritative theory sources. NOAA's f-plane/beta-plane description does not establish whether f is evaluated at each grid latitude, a reference latitude or through another discretization. Gulf latitudes avoid the equator, but that does not justify assuming globally constant f.

At most, a future qualified calculation would be a **MAPPED_GEOSTROPHIC_FIELD_DERIVATIVE_DIAGNOSTIC**. It would not establish total-surface-current convergence, vertical velocity, downwelling, fish presence, aggregation, catch probability or Opportunity eligibility. Unequal inward magnitudes do not by themselves invalidate inward geometry; shear and edges need not be mutually exclusive with it. None of those statements qualifies the present candidate as detection.

Preserve uncertainty states separately; a single Boolean remains **BOOLEAN_TOO_LOSSY**. Ocean Physics consumption stays **NOT_YET_QUALIFIED**. No new state enum, threshold or contract is approved.

# Pelora product constitution

Canonical repository-level product authority. This bootstrap records the founder's locked governance instruction; it is not implementation or release authorization. Detailed contracts remain authoritative within their qualified scope. Material amendments require human approval and a decision-log entry.

## Purpose and captain authority

Pelora is an Ocean Intelligence platform.

“The ocean speaks first. Pelora translates. The captain decides.”

Pelora helps captains understand the ocean and make better decisions. Captain judgment remains final. Pelora is not a catch-report popularity map, a fish-presence predictor or a guaranteed-success predictor.

## Product and map language

Ocean Signals and Opportunities are distinct. Strong Ocean Signals may exist without qualifying as Opportunities. Places, Ocean Signals and Opportunities retain distinct map languages. Catch/community density must never determine Pelora map hierarchy, verification, confidence or rank.

Reuse [Governed Places](Governed_Places_v1.md) and [map visual foundation](Pelora_Map_Visual_Foundation_v1.md). A Place's existence and an environmental observation do not grant Opportunity eligibility. Science and ranking permission follow [science governance](PELORA_SCIENCE_GOVERNANCE.md).

Competitor monitoring protects differentiation; it does not authorize copying competitor features or changing Pelora's direction.

## Geographic scalability

Gulf of Mexico is deployment/validation region #1, not the architecture boundary. Roadmap direction remains Gulf → U.S. Atlantic → later offshore regions. Core engines/contracts must be region-neutral unless science/provider behavior requires a regional adapter. Do not hardcode Gulf-specific assumptions into generic infrastructure. Region expansion does not transfer source qualification automatically.

## Prior vision and authority

[SmartCharts vision](SmartCharts-Vision.md) remains historical alpha context. Its Smart Charts naming, initial corridor, event target and feature list do not authorize current scope, scientific claims or deployment. This constitution governs current product decisions; the historical vision is not a competing canon.

See [decision log](PELORA_DECISION_LOG.md) for unresolved naming/scope questions and [beta board](Beta_Command_Board_Current_State_v1.md) for evidence-backed completion status.

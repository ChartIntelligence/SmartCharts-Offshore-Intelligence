## 3. Physical quantity and interpretation

**A — [A1: Marshall & Plumb, chapter 6](https://weathertank.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/2017/04/chap6.pdf), §6.3.** In local Cartesian horizontal coordinates, horizontal divergence is `D = ∂u/∂x + ∂v/∂y` (s⁻¹); negative D describes horizontal convergence. Incompressible continuity relates D to `−∂w/∂z`, not directly to w. **I:** vertical velocity requires vertical support and a boundary condition; a single horizontal product cannot determine downwelling, vertical circulation or transport. On a geographic grid, an estimator must address spherical metrics/local-coordinate approximation explicitly; degrees are not meters.

**A — [A2: Marshall & Plumb, chapter 7](https://weathertank.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/2017/04/chap7.pdf), §7.1.** Ideal constant-f geostrophic velocity is horizontally nondivergent. **I:** this is a critical constraint on the actual product. With constant f, `u_g=−(g/f)η_y`, `v_g=(g/f)η_x` gives cancellation of mixed derivatives. With spatially varying f the cancellation is not generally exact; grid metrics, discrete differencing, mapping and rounding also matter. Do not assert every gridded geostrophic divergence is identically zero, or interpret a numerical residual as total surface-current convergence. The product omits unrepresented ageostrophic contributions; adding a derivative alone cannot recover them.

**S — [S2: McWilliams, 2016](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.2016.0117), §5b.** Frontogenesis can involve geostrophic shear, deformation and an ageostrophic secondary circulation with convergence. These structures can coexist. **I:** Pelora's shear/edge overlap is not inherently contradictory, but shared observations are not independent corroboration. A speed edge is not automatically a density front. The paper does not validate Pelora's geometry, thresholds or this product's ability to resolve those processes.

**I.** Persistence is separate from the sign of a spatial derivative at a specified time. Repetition can support stability/feature identity, but is not mathematically necessary to define a present-state divergence. No duration is selected. Likewise unequal inward magnitudes do not invalidate negative divergence: sign depends on spatial differences, not equality of vector speeds. Counting three inward vectors alone does not guarantee negative net flux/derivative; an uncounted outward contribution can dominate. This is an algebraic limitation, not a new runtime test or new threshold.

## 4. Discrete evidence and uncertainty

**S — [S3: Fornberg, 1988](https://www.colorado.edu/amath/sites/default/files/attached-files/mathcomp_88_fd_formulas.pdf).** Derivative approximations depend on node positions and the available stencil; centered, one-sided and unequal-spacing constructions are mathematically possible with different accuracy. **I:** the following is a support/rank analysis, not a selected Pelora estimator.

| Evidence state | Scientifically supportable interpretation / limit (**I**, from spatial-derivative definition and geometry) |
|---|---|
| Four directional vectors, common temporal support, resolved distinct positions | Both directional derivative contributions are potentially estimable at a reviewed scale; physical significance still unqualified for this product. |
| Center missing, four directions intact | Center is not mathematically mandatory for opposing-pair differences; do not reject solely for absent center. |
| One direction missing, valid center present | A one-sided/unequal stencil may be possible, with different support/error; it is not the complete symmetric stencil. |
| One direction and center missing | Three noncollinear full vectors can fit an affine field, but that imposes an unqualified spatial model; no general model-free local derivative is established. |
| Opposite pair missing | Remaining cardinal points plus center are collinear; one horizontal derivative direction lacks direct support. No general full 2-D divergence. |
| Missing represented time | No temporal alignment/currentness claim; current default chain rejects available untimed points. |
| Different represented dates | Not one instantaneous spatial field without a justified alignment/stationarity model. Freshness alone is insufficient. |
| Unavailable or rejected/future evidence | No positive convergence claim from those values; remaining evidence must be evaluated as its actual reduced geometry. |
| Same cell repeated | Repetition adds no spatial derivative support. |

**I.** At a coast/mask, do not bridge land or silently fill missing samples. Interpolation can construct a field but adds no independent observations. Spatial smoothing, truncation, snapping, four-decimal component/projection rounding and effective product resolution affect derivatives. These limitations cannot be cured by simply asking ERDDAP for closer coordinates.

**A — [A3: NIST TN1297 Appendix A](https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297/nist-tn-1297-appendix-law-propagation-uncertainty).** Uncertainty propagation includes sensitivities and covariance, not just separate input errors. **I:** differentiating noisy velocities involves inverse-distance sensitivity. A later qualification needs component errors/correlations, mapping and representativeness error, time/position uncertainty, discretization and rounding effects. Adjacent optimal-interpolation cells cannot be presumed independent. No confidence formula, numerical uncertainty, false-positive probability or detection threshold is adopted.

**U.** Reviewed exact-endpoint metadata exposes velocity/fill validity but not these uncertainty inputs. Finite numbers and exact captures do not suffice for significance. The result is `CURRENT_QUALITY_EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT` for a detection claim and `UNCERTAINTY_QUALIFICATION_REQUIRED`. Product-specific coastal/open-ocean error statistics remain unresolved; no HF-radar or other product error numbers are borrowed.

**I/D.** A spatial derivative compares one temporal support. Independently fresh daily values can still mix evolving fields. `DEDICATED_TEMPORAL_COHERENCE_POLICY_REQUIRED`: bind a common represented product frame/support or scientifically qualify another alignment approach. This is a requirement for later design, not an adopted maximum separation. Same timestamp is necessary evidence for common-frame selection but does not by itself establish the product's averaging kernel or release consistency.

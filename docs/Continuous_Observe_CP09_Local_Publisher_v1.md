## Durable ownership and atomic publication

`cp09.configurations` and append-only `cp09.disables` retain authority. `cp09.jobs` has deterministic context/boundary identity and the existing governed cycle ID; attempt/fence/capability identity is separate. `cp09.attempts`, `cp09.freezes`, `cp09.submissions` and `cp09.completions` are immutable. Terminal job state is protected. The configuration disable transition cancels nonterminal fixture work without erasing frozen evidence or submissions. Tests disable their configurations at shutdown; earlier unqualified controlled configurations were disabled append-only, with all records retained.

`cp09.worker(text,text,uuid,text)` provides narrow list/enumerate/inspect/claim/freeze/prepare/release/finalize operations. Leases and monotonically increasing fences serialize per job. Configuration concurrency/retry limits are enforced in PostgreSQL; freeze/submission conflicts fail closed. There is no observation claim reuse. No transaction is kept open while reading CP-07, waiting, collecting or evaluating.

Finalization locks the publication job, configuration and CP-08 context, samples database time **after the locks**, validates current capability/fence/lease/terminal/config authority, invokes the internal CP-08 transition on the exact persisted submission, verifies exact readback, writes completion and consumes the job in one transaction. This is independent of constraint timing. Rollback restores recoverability. A lease valid at the atomic terminal transition is consumed; expiration after that transition does not undo accepted state. Obsolete attempts cannot finalize, including a lease that expires while waiting for the context lock.

The original CP-08 implementation becomes owner-only `cp08.submit_internal(text)`. The worker-visible `cp08.submit(text)` locks the context and rejects managed CP-09 slots, preventing legacy bypass. Unmanaged CP-08 submissions retain their original semantics and revision checks. For managed cycles, explicit governed corrections/withdrawals remain admin-controlled calls to the same internal CP-08 routine; an ordinary scheduler cannot issue them. A completed job is not reclaimed and replay cannot resurrect a withdrawn head. CP-08 lineage and one active head remain unchanged.

After uncertainty the publisher first inspects authoritative completion/submission state. Committed exact state is reconciled without another revision. Otherwise it reuses the immutable freeze, and a prepared submission is retried byte-for-byte under a new valid claim. An expired prepared completed result cannot be silently rewritten to fit a deadline; finalization rejects it, bounded retries eventually record failure and preserve the unaccepted artifacts. Exhausted/disabled jobs need not fabricate a CP-08 scientific publication; the ledger explicitly records their state.

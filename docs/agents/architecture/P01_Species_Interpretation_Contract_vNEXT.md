## Minimal versioned species-specific interpretation contract

Proposed pelora-species-interpretation-design-vnext-1, not runtime schema or scientific evaluator. Shared neutral core owns identity/reference validation, assessment/freeze/history resolution semantics, exact numeric-source support and consumer boundaries; species adapter owns only scientifically approved claim semantics. Region/season/life-stage applicability explicit, no Gulf hardcoding or copied Blue Marlin thresholds in global core. Region availability/adapters remain separate from generic infrastructure.

Every proposed claim must have:

1. Exact species/candidate/static-parent/context identity and ordered assessment/cohort; immutable evidence and source/product/run/revision parents actually supplied.
2. Versioned scientific relationship/approval packet plus independent validation record, approved meaning and permitted product/source classes, applicable region/season/life-stage/diel/depth domain. Literature citation/document QA alone is insufficient.
3. Required consumed payload and units, time/support/possession/cutoff, spatial/native/candidate correspondence, quality/uncertainty and missingness. Dependency requirements are claim-specific; do not demand every future layer for unrelated valid branches.
4. Interpreted environmental meaning and explicit limitations; separate from habitat eligibility/ranking permission. No fish presence/absence/catch-probability from SST color or missing observation.
5. Output reference binding original producer identities, actual eligibility/adequacy/confidence/relationship/reasons, exclusion/ranking permissions and delivery/evaluation state. No replacement identities, score/confidence recalculation, sorted repaired results or completeness from array lengths.
6. Immutable as-used input/history set and original assessment; replay same references, no new evidence/revision or receipt attached after evaluation to make it appear supported. Retrospective audit is separately referenced, never overwrites prior assessment.

Not a new evaluator/classifier/ranker. A future adapter may compose approved native functions under reviewed inputs, retaining one scientific implementation; no parallel formulas. No change to current production gates/scores/confidence/source admission.

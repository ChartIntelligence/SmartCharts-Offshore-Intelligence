### Failure states and explicit repair boundaries

Existing V3 AVAILABLE-empty needs a governed successful-resolution reference. UNAVAILABLE is not empty success. INVALID blocks evaluation/completion/pointer advancement. **Proposed adapter mapping:** absent/read-outage/unknown-required-authority retain distinct reasons; corrupt/conflicting references are invalid; valid retained no-pixel/failure stays source evidence. Too few admissible supports yields insufficient-history for its dependent consumer, never invented concentration zero, confidence, eligibility or governed-zero. Current evidence remains governed independently; no blanket historical requirement for every family is added. Existing evaluation-state producer owns available/partial/unavailable/governed-zero.

Cutoff counterexamples are conditional synthetic resolver/consumer reachability, not demonstrated production leakage. Retrieval filtering alone cannot repair consumed future leaves, unknown support or vintage. Derived-finiteness counterexamples likewise demonstrate finite-endpoint overflow, not observed provider extremes. Time checks do not fix arithmetic. Repair handoff/exact selection first, then finite derived/dependent facts without rewriting source or classifications. Low-level SST/current examples do not become established default-production defects. Clarity labels, numeric-string/fill/legacy coordinate issues retain separate classification. Convergence/Ocean Physics and derivative science remain quarantined; no repairs occurred here.

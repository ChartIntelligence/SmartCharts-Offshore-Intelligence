## Eligibility gates remain distinct

Each row records the logical prerequisites:

`EXACT_CONTENT_IDENTITY → AVAILABILITY_BY_ASSESSMENT → TEMPORAL_SUPPORT → FAMILY/SOURCE ADMISSIBILITY → REVISION AUTHORITY → CONSUMER LOOKBACK → ORDERING → GAP POLICY → SUFFICIENT HISTORY → CONSUMER ARITHMETIC`

This is a contract dependency model, not an executable selector. Failure or unresolved authority at a required gate cannot be bypassed by successful arithmetic. Existing typed reference/sample validators supply identity mechanisms, but their resolution and relation to active legacy rows still require authority. Trusted exact `receivedAt <= assessmentAt` is necessary for as-of possession. First-ever receipt is not required; represented time alone is insufficient.

The new tests apply that availability condition to each of the 16 active requirements using the existing qualification oracle. These are 16 checks of a shared necessary rule, not 16 end-to-end scientific-consumer qualifications. They neither invoke quarantined convergence science nor assert that one fixture qualifies all families.

## Identity, receipts and revisions

A qualifying pre-assessment receipt may establish availability; a later repeat does not make another observation. The test confirms two receipt events bind one evidence identity.

Same-support competing revisions remain distinct content but do not create elapsed observation time. The SST control returns unavailable for two differently valued revisions at the same time, with zero represented duration. Mechanical preservation of historical as-used records is not retrospective scientific requalification.
